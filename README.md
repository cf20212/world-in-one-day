# world-in-one-day

THE WORLD IN ONE DAY — a newspaper front-page poster summing up the world news of Sunday 22 November 2026 in one image: the COP31 climate talks collapsing in Antalya, a Russian drone strike levelling a Kyiv-region apartment block, US Democrats reaching 258 House seats, Iran's strike on a Bahrain industrial site, and BepiColombo entering orbit around Mercury after its eight-year journey. The poster (1024×1024 PNG) ships in the repo; a small Python program copies it to /output/entry.png.

## Run

`python3 main.py` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (it honours `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set).
